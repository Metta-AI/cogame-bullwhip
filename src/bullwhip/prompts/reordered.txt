Each week you receive a downstream order, ship available inventory, and place ONE upstream order. Units you cannot fill become BACKLOG and remain owed until shipped.
The upstream stage sees your order NEXT week. After shipping, goods need 2 weeks to reach you. An order placed this week arrives in 3 weeks at the earliest, or later if the supplier is backlogged.
Customer demand stays hidden and is roughly steady but may change level during the game. Over-ordering into backlog creates a wave that returns weeks later as your own excess inventory: the bullwhip effect.
